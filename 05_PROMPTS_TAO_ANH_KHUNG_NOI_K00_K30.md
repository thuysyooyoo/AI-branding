# PROMPT TẠO 31 KHUNG ẢNH NỐI CẢNH

## Cách dùng

Mỗi prompt bên dưới phải được nối sau **MASTER IMAGE PROMPT**. Tạo đúng tỷ lệ 16:9. Khung K01 vừa là ảnh cuối của S01 vừa là ảnh đầu của S02; quy tắc này tiếp tục đến K30.

## MASTER IMAGE PROMPT

`Use the uploaded character image as the exact identity reference and the uploaded scenery image as the exact illustration-style reference. Preserve the same face, hairstyle, apparent age, body proportions, outfit, accessories, color palette, line quality and painting medium in every image. Hand-painted editorial 2.5D illustration, textured gouache and colored-pencil details, subtle paper grain, cinematic lighting, teal-blue European logistics environments with warm amber accents, coherent scale and geography, 16:9, 4K, clean lower-third space for Vietnamese subtitles. No readable text, no logos, no watermark, no extra fingers, no facial drift, no outfit changes, no photorealism.`
